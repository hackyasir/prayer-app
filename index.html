About Us

Established in 1989 by Maulana Shah Ahmad Noorani Siddiqui.

World Islamic Mission Canada is a religious organization adhering to the Ahl-ul-Sunnah religious mainstream. Our beliefs and teachings are grounded in the Holy Quran and the traditions (sunnahs) of the last Prophet of Allah Almighty, peace be upon him.

Since our establishment, we have been actively involved in serving the community — through the construction of Masjid Noor-UL-Haram, Da'wah efforts, conferences, food bank services, and providing spiritual guidance to people of all ages.
